# What's New — May 2026

## Script Mode
More ways to shape bigger scripts with better context and less friction.

- **Research before writing** — Run a quick research pass before generating a script, then use the research brief to shape the draft.
- **Longform scripts** — Use structured longform generation for bigger episodes, with progress tracking and cancel support instead of waiting on one long browser request.
- **Long scripts that match the promise** — The Long script option now aims for a fuller episode-length draft instead of stopping after a short exchange.
- **Drafts that stay stable** — Script Mode autosave skips unchanged drafts and fails softer when account settings are temporarily unavailable.

## Visualizer
More control over what your audience sees while the conversation unfolds.

- **Visual Direction controls** — Choose whether the Visualizer follows the conversation, your own search terms, or a blend of both.
- **Capture text overlay** — Add a short on-screen note card beside the title and subtitles for recordings or demos.
- **OBS overlay mode** — Open a transparent Visualizer overlay for screen recording tools, with optional green or black background modes.
- **Emoji accents** — Add local, privacy-safe emoji accents based on the conversation topic, with style and placement controls.
- **Klipy motion visuals** — Switch the Visualizer into motion mode with Klipy GIF/video results and visible attribution.
- **Better visual freshness** — Bot lines update the Visualizer topic buffer more reliably as audio is emitted.
- **Cleaner capture subtitles** — Internal RSS/autoprompt instructions are filtered out of viewer-facing subtitles.
- **Less repetitive image selection** — Unsplash results avoid recently used images for the same search when alternatives are available.

## Character Creator
Character edits are smoother, especially on mobile.

- **Character edits that stick** — Applying a character now uses the name, avatar, personality, provider, and voice currently selected in the modal.
- **Better mobile saving** — Save/apply buttons are protected from double-taps, and provider changes no longer snap back while editing.

## Conversation quality
Co-hosts respond more cleanly to who said what.

- **Fewer duplicate co-host replies** — Stronger safeguards reduce repeated or double-sent bot responses.
- **Better direct-name routing** — If you ask for a specific co-host by custom name, the right co-host is more likely to answer.
- **Clearer speaker attribution** — Co-hosts better distinguish host speech from co-host speech in recent context.
- **Stuck response recovery** — If a co-host response gets stuck, the system recovers faster instead of staying blocked for close to a minute.

## Topics & RSS
Autoprompt and RSS sessions are easier to follow.

- **RSS topics with a visible countdown** — Starting RSS auto-read now shows clearer countdown/status feedback.
- **Next topic preview** — Before the first RSS topic is injected, the mixer can show what's coming next instead of only saying it is waiting.
- **Clearer autoprompt status** — The mixer can explain whether it's waiting for audio, loading RSS, injecting, or stopped.

## Model choices
More options for balancing speed, quality, and cost.

- **DeepSeek V4 Flash option** — Added a new budget LLM option for users who want another fast, lower-cost model choice.
