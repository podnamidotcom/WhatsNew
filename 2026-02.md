# What's New — February 2026

## Try Before You Sign Up
Easier ways to experience PodNami without creating an account.

- **Studio Preview Mode** — Public demo at [/studio/demo](https://podnami.com/studio/demo). Turn limits, session expiry, and clear "Preview" badge. Try the full experience before signing up.
- **Shareable clips from preview** — Record and share a clip during the demo. Share links on social media and invite others to try PodNami.
- **Preview avatar selection** — Choose avatars during the demo (session-only, no account required).

## Recording & Sharing
Better recording, exporting, and sharing of your conversations.

- **Shareable audio clips** — Record short clips and share them publicly. Each clip gets a shareable link and social share buttons.
- **Clips for all users** — Clip sharing for all users, not just preview. Plan-based limits and expiry (e.g., Pro 30 days, Studio 90 days).
- **Listen before you share** — Preview your clip before sharing or downloading.
- **Expanded social sharing** — Share clips to Twitter/X, Reddit, Facebook, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Threads, Bluesky, and Copy Link.
- **Improved expired clip pages** — Clear messaging when a clip has expired, with CTAs to try PodNami or sign up.

## Language Lab
Foreign language practice with AI co-hosts.

- **Language Lab launch** — Dedicated rooms for Spanish, French, Italian, German, and Chinese with language-specific STT, TTS, and LLM settings.
- **Co-host display names per lab** — Custom names for co-hosts in each language (e.g., "Miguel" and "Elena" in Spanish).
- **Voice labels in mixer** — Clear labels for which voice is used in each lab.
- **Chinese Language Lab** — Mandarin support via Amazon Nova Lite.
- **Dashboard quick action** — One-click access to Language Lab from the dashboard.
- **Front-page CTA** — Language Lab highlighted on the main landing page.

## Geo-Personalization
Location-aware conversations without exposing your exact location.

- **Location-aware AI** — Co-hosts can reference your city, country, and local time when relevant.
- **Privacy toggle** — Turn geo-personalization on or off in Settings.

## Personalization & Characters
More control over how your co-hosts look, sound, and behave.

- **Single co-host mode** — Choose one co-host or two. Solo mode adjusts prompts and behavior for a one-on-one conversation.
- **Custom names improvements** — Easier to assign custom names to co-hosts from Settings, with "Set Male" / "Set Female" actions.
- **ElevenLabs in Character Creator** — Use ElevenLabs voice IDs when creating characters (Pro+).
- **Character creator reliability fixes** — Better dropdown loading, preview behavior, and link visibility.
- **Custom personality recovery** — If your active custom personality disappeared from pickers, it now shows correctly again.

## Conversation Quality
Improvements to how conversations feel and flow.

- **Echo suppression** — Server-side echo gate reduces bots responding to their own audio when it bleeds through your mic.
- **Smarter interruptions** — Better handling when you interrupt a co-host; playback stops and responses are more coherent.
- **Conversation context fix** — Co-hosts keep better track of recent turns and topic changes.
- **More natural topic close-outs** — Topic transitions sound more natural instead of formulaic.

## Audio & Calibration
Better audio handling and calibration for different setups.

- **Audio setup profiles** — Choose your setup (headset, headphones+mic, laptop speakers, external speakers) for more accurate calibration.
- **Tune AutoMute calibration** — Calibrate echo suppression for your environment.
- **Agent thinking indicators** — Per-agent visual feedback showing thinking, buffering, and speaking states with animated avatar borders.

## Onboarding & Billing
Smoother signup and upgrade flows.

- **Sign up with plan** — Choose Pro or Studio on the landing page and flow straight through registration to Stripe checkout.
- **Launch pricing** — Launch pricing with automatic transition to full price after the launch period.
- **Influencer promo codes** — Promo URLs for partners with extended discounts.
- **Dashboard billing access** — Manage billing and subscription from the dashboard.

## Reliability & Polish
Fixes and improvements that make the product more reliable.

- **Typed input for paid users** — Typed input no longer incorrectly blocked for Pro/Studio users.
- **Preview turn consumption** — Preview turns are not consumed when the mic is muted.
- **Preview Inworld voices** — Preview mode uses Inworld voices (Edward/Ashley) as intended.
- **API keys "session expired" fix** — Resolved long-standing API keys loading and session issues.
- **Free tier upgrade message** — Users at the free limit see a clear upgrade message.
