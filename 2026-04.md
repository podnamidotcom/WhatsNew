# What's New — April 2026

## Script Mode & show prep
Plan your episode like a producer, then step into the studio when you're ready.

- **Explore Script Mode from one link** — The Script Mode page on the site explains the workflow for everyone; when your plan includes it, the same place becomes the full editor to generate, tweak, and send lines to the studio.
- **Flow to Script (Pro & Studio)** — In the studio, capture the final transcript into a running buffer, pause anytime, then open Script Mode with those lines — no race with a timer that throws away your take.
- **Bring a script on stage** — Send a script to the studio and get a side panel that follows the show: see what's playing, fix drift with resync when the queue moves, and use Continue when the script says it's your turn.
- **Remix with AI** — Start from an import or from Flow to Script, then ask for a fresh pass that keeps your material but reshapes it into a cleaner episode — same credits idea as generating new.
- **Read along without losing the rundown** — With the script panel open and expanded, jumping in while a co-host is talking is gentler on playback so you're not always clearing the whole queue by accident.
- **Handoff that respects your latest work** — Coming from the mixer with Flow to Script, your new capture wins over an older saved draft so the import screen matches what you just did.

## Recording & sharing
Easier to sound professional when you share.

- **Clip pages that feel finished** — People who open your link get a simple page to hit play. The "save file and post it yourself" flow stays with you in the mixer.
- **Links that don't confuse people** — If someone opens a bare audio or video file in the browser, we bring them to the full clip page instead of leaving them staring at a tiny player.
- **Video audiograms for social** — After you share a clip, Free and Pro see a clear path to Studio if you want tall video for Reels, Shorts, or TikTok. Studio and Enterprise keep the full creator workflow.
- **Audiograms with personality** — Studio and Enterprise can dial in the look — energy, waveform style, and colors — before you export.

## Conversation polish
Small things that make the show feel tighter.

- **Captions that match the moment** — The live text tracks what you're actually hearing, not an early draft that already changed.
- **Space to finish your thought** — Co-hosts are less likely to jump in while you're still talking.

## Studio tools
When you're editing for real.

- **Multitrack recording** — Your mic, each co-host, and the soundboard save as separate tracks in one download for your editor.
- **Recording library** — Find past clips and multitrack sessions in Settings, grab another copy, or clean up what you don't need.
