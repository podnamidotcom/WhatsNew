# What's New — July 2026

## Tarot Readings
Something new: ask a question and let a co-host read the cards for you.

_📍 Find it in Dashboard → Tarot_

- **A whole new kind of session** — Start a reading and your co-host shuffles, deals a spread, and talks you through the cards out loud, one at a time.
- **Save your reading** — Keep a reading after the session ends, with its cards and the full transcript, so you can come back and revisit it anytime.
- **Watch the spread come to life** — Cards land face-down with a shuffle and a soft glow while your reader gathers their thoughts, then turn face-up right as they start to speak.
- **Cards that stay out of your way** — The spread sits over the studio without blocking your controls, and the Hide button stays easy to reach even on a big layout.
- **Readings that flow** — Your reading comes through as one smooth take instead of choppy pieces, and every reading stays its own — no leftover details from an earlier question.

## Response Length
Decide how much your co-hosts say.

_📍 Set it in Settings → General_

- **Choose Verbose or Sharp** — One setting controls how long your co-hosts' replies run, across everything you do. Keep the fuller replies with Verbose, or switch to Sharp for quick, to-the-point answers.

## Faster responses
Less waiting, more talking.

_💡 Choose the Claude Haiku 4.5 or Gemini Flash models for the snappiest replies_

- **Co-hosts start sooner** — Your co-hosts begin speaking as soon as they have their first thought, instead of waiting for the whole reply to be ready.
- **Quicker to jump in** — Co-hosts notice when you've finished talking faster, so there's less of a pause before they answer.
- **Tidier queue** — A long reading now shows up as a single item in What's Next instead of a stack of tiny rows.

## Conversation quality
Smoother, more natural exchanges.

_✨ Works automatically — nothing to switch on_

- **Your words always land** — Saying something with a word like "wait" or "actually" in it is no longer mistaken for an interruption and skipped.
- **More natural back-and-forth** — Fewer repeated replies, and co-hosts keep the conversation moving forward instead of circling back to something they already answered.
- **Right name, cleaner voice** — Co-host names update right away after you change a character, and voices repeat themselves less at the end of a line.
