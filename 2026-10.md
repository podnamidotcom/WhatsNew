# What's New — October 2026

## Signature Voices
Voices made for PodNami — and you can request your own.

_📍 Pick them in Settings, or see them on the [Signature Voices page](https://podnami.com/signature-voices) — Studio_

- **Voices made here, not just licensed** — It started with eight Swedish voices built for Language Lab. Those are now available as PodNami Signature Voices.
- **Request a voice that does not exist yet** — Describe the voice you want — an old-school radio announcer, a sarcastic gym coach, something nobody has thought of — and [submit a request](https://podnami.com/voice-request).
- **Describe it without writing English** — The request form uses menus rather than a free-text box, so you can describe a Swedish or German voice without having to describe it *in* English.
- **Your own voices are marked as yours** — The picker shows which voices are yours, which are house voices, and which one is currently in use.

## Speech Recognition
It hears you properly now.

_📍 Use the mic and language controls in the studio_

- **Your language is settled once, not guessed every sentence** — Language was being detected separately for each sentence, so a single Swedish session could come back as a mix of Danish, Dutch, Norwegian and German. You now choose your conversation language and it stays put for the session.
- **Auto-detect has been removed** — Guessing per sentence turned out to be the problem itself, not a feature. Choosing is more predictable, especially outside English.
- **Your co-hosts are told which language the show is in** — Previously they had to work it out from what you said.
- **The first syllable is no longer clipped on mobile** — The phone recorder was cutting the start of what you said before it was ever transcribed. Fixed — which quietly explains a lot of mishearings.
- **Speech is no longer discarded in non-Latin scripts** — A safety check meant to filter noise could remove real speech instead of flagging it. It now annotates rather than deletes.
- **Cleaner audio reaching the transcriber** — Audio is converted before transcription, which improves accuracy regardless of which provider is used.

## Faster Conversations
Less waiting between turns.

_✨ Works automatically — nothing to switch on_

- **Noticeably quicker on phones** — Around three quarters of a second less waiting every time you finish speaking. Not as fast as we want yet, but a real improvement in back-and-forth conversation.
- **Co-hosts speak a little more briskly** — Less dead air between turns.

## Privacy
What happens to your conversations, in plain terms.

_📍 Control memory in Settings → Memory_

- **Conversation text stays out of server logs** — And there is now an automated check that fails if it is ever reintroduced, so it does not depend on anyone remembering.
- **Turn memory off entirely** — Switch off "Remember past sessions" and nothing from your conversations is written to disk for that feature.
- **A privacy policy that matches the code** — Rewritten to describe what PodNami actually does with your data, rather than what it was intended to do.

## Language Lab
Stop having your mistakes quietly corrected.

_📍 Find it in Language Lab_

- **Much less silent correction** — Transcription was tidying up learners' grammar, which rather defeats the point of practising. It now preserves what you actually said far more often. Better, with more work still to do here.

## Fixes You May Have Noticed
Small things that were more annoying than they should have been.

_✨ Already live_

- **Co-hosts no longer read error messages aloud** — If you use your own API keys, a key problem could be spoken by a co-host as if it were dialogue. Fixed.
- **Producer notes are not mistaken for your own speech** — On mobile, injected notes could appear as though you had said them.
- **"Save + Apply" has an honest name** — The button saved your settings but never applied them. The label now describes what it really does.
- **Voices show their names, not their IDs** — Designed voices were displaying raw technical identifiers in Settings and in the studio.
- **A voice picker you can actually scan** — Grouped and compact instead of one enormous list of near-identical cards, and the "in use" marker no longer duplicates.
- **Changing your custom name takes one step** — At the one-name limit it used to refuse the save and tell you to delete the old name first. It now simply replaces it, and says so (Pro).
- **A saveable language default** — The language dropdown's own default value could not be saved.

## And more
Multiple performance, reliability, and privacy improvements behind the scenes, including a substantial reduction in how much of your content the system touches at all, while using [PodNami.com](https://podnami.com).
