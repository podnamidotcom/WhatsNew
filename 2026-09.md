# What's New — September 2026

## Ask Producer
Turn a topic into a show brief.

_📍 Find it in the studio's Pre-Show Brief panel — Pro & Studio_

- **Get topic ideas before you start** — Give PodNami a direction and Ask Producer can suggest angles you might want to cover.
- **Build a usable brief** — Pick the angles you like and turn them into a Pre-Show Brief your co-hosts can actually work through during the conversation.
- **Ground it in live sources** — Ask Producer can pull in current information and add sources, so the brief is not just a generic outline.
- **Regenerate when it is not right** — Ask for more topics, different angles, or a rewritten brief without starting over.

## Pre-Show Brief Rundowns
Run a more planned show.

_📍 Set it up in the studio's Pre-Show Brief panel_

- **Start from the page you choose** — Begin a rundown from the page you are editing, instead of always starting at Page 1.
- **Shorter segment options** — Flip every 5 or 10 minutes if you want a faster show pace.
- **Move pages around** — Reorder your brief pages so the live running order matches the show you want to do.
- **Cleaner transitions** — Co-hosts now do a better job moving to the next segment without reopening the show or drifting back to the old topic.

## No-Host Show
Let the co-hosts carry the room while you step back.

_📍 Find it in the studio's Co-Host Show panel_

- **Step away without the empty-chair feeling** — Turn on No-Host Show and your co-hosts stop constantly handing the conversation back to you.
- **Give the show a name** — Add a show name and the co-hosts can open the session without you typing a starter prompt.
- **Start fresh** — Starting a no-host rundown now clears old queued audio and old conversation context, so the new show does not drag in the previous one.
- **Choose how they continue** — Keep them on the current thread, bridge into a new topic, or use the brief as the source of topics.

## Co-Host Memory
They can come back to the question you missed.

_✨ Works automatically — nothing to switch on_

- **Open questions do not disappear as easily** — If a co-host asks you something and you never answer, they can bring that question back later.
- **Works with your co-hosts** — The same behavior works with built-in and custom personalities.
- **Still lightweight** — This is not a giant memory bank. It is focused on the live conversation and the questions still hanging in the air.

## More Natural Conversations
Less panel talk, more back-and-forth.

_✨ Works automatically — nothing to switch on_

- **Shorter, more human turns** — Co-hosts are better at giving a quick reaction instead of turning every reply into a mini essay.
- **Fewer stock openings** — Cleaned up repeated phrases like generic acknowledgements and interview-style handbacks.
- **Better topic bridges** — When Co-Host Show brings in a new subject, the jump can feel more connected to what was just being discussed.
- **Interruptions land better** — Cutting in now stops more of the in-flight response instead of letting the old thought keep going.
- **Cleaner spoken output** — Fixed cases where internal notes, stage directions, or reasoning text could be spoken aloud.

## Speech and Language
Better handling for real microphones and real speakers.

_📍 Use the mic and language controls in the studio_

- **Language auto-detect** — Speech recognition can now start from Auto instead of forcing every new speaker through English.
- **Wrong-script noise gets filtered** — Short sounds like "hmm" are less likely to turn into stray text in the wrong alphabet.
- **The mic recovers more gracefully** — Improved recovery when the microphone stream gets stuck or the selected device changes.
