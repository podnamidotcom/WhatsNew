# What's New — March 2026

## Recording & Sharing
Better recording, exporting, and sharing of your conversations.

- **Background recording processing** — Recording conversion no longer freezes the UI. You can keep talking or start a new recording while your clip is being processed.
- **Silence removal** — Optional "Remove silence" checkbox trims long pauses from your recordings for cleaner, shorter exports.
- **Silence removal in clip share panel** — "Remove silence" checkbox directly in the clip share panel so you can trim pauses before sharing.
- **Social clip auto-save on disconnect** — If you disconnect while recording a clip, it auto-saves so you can share it before leaving.
- **TikTok and Instagram share buttons** — Share clips to TikTok and Instagram (copy link for captions).
- **Better link previews for shared clips** — Shared clip links show a branded image when posted on social media.
- **Share counts in My Clips** — See how many times each clip has been shared (e.g. "12 plays · 5 shares").
- **Unsaved recording warning** — The browser warns you before closing if you have a recording you haven't downloaded or shared.
- **Encoding progress toast** — Visual feedback when your recording is being converted in the background.

## Transcription
Clearer feedback when you speak.

- **Transcription when muted** — When AutoMute is on and you speak, you see "👤 You (muted): [your words]" instead of a generic message, so you know your words were captured.

## Conversation Quality
Improvements to how conversations feel and flow.

- **Transcription debounce** — Rapid speech is merged into a single turn instead of triggering multiple responses.
- **Topic transition improvements** — When a new topic is injected, co-hosts move on instead of circling back to old topics.
- **Topic queue clearing** — Clear Topics and Stop buttons now properly clear the textarea and persist the empty state.
- **LLM output cleanup** — Co-hosts no longer repeat internal speaker labels or talk as if an absent co-host is present.

## Personalization
More control over how your co-hosts behave.

- **Solo mode prompt fix** — In single co-host mode, the AI now knows it's a one-on-one conversation and behaves accordingly.

## Mixer UX & Controls
Clearer interface and more control over playback.

- **Pause After button** — Let the current audio finish, then pause automatically.
- **Simple / Advanced view** — Toggle between a simplified view for new users and full controls for power users.
- **Queue control fixes** — Skip and Clear now stop currently playing audio on the client. Queue tooltips describe each button.

## Pre-Show Brief & Topic Queue
Better preparation and control over conversation topics.

- **Pre-Show Brief** — Add guest info, talking points, and episode goals before or during a conversation. Co-hosts use this context in their responses (Pro+).
- **Topic queue # comments** — Lines starting with # in the topic list are section headers or notes; they're not sent to the AI.

## Credits & Billing
New credit system: add credits when you want us to cover AI and voice, or plug in your own provider keys and skip that side of the meter.

- **Your own API keys** — In Settings you can use the AI and voice providers you already pay for directly. PodNami credits are only used when you're leaning on our hosted access — not when those steps run on your keys.
- **New pricing** — $4.95 for Pro and $9.95 for Studio. New prices include 100 credits (Pro) and 200 credits (Studio).
- **Add credits in two clicks** — Right from the dashboard.

## Account Management
Manage your account with one click right from the dashboard.

## Reliability & Polish
Fixes and improvements that make the product more reliable.

- **ElevenLabs German prosody** — Improved multilingual model for more natural German delivery.
- **Pause button (idle mode)** — Fully pause co-hosts (no listening, no responding, no bot-to-bot).
- **Stop button in queue row** — Quick access to stop playback without scrolling.
