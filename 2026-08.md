## Smoother conversations
A long list of fixes that add up to a better back-and-forth.

_✨ Works automatically — nothing to switch on_

- **Co-hosts remember the thread** — They keep track of what was already said, so they stop circling back to points they've made and stop losing the thread when you change the subject.
- **Questions get answered** — When one co-host asks the other something directly, it lands far more reliably instead of going unanswered.
- **The right name, every time** — Fixed a problem where a co-host could occasionally use the wrong name.
- **No more stray stage directions** — Cleaned up rare cases where a mood word or a bit of internal formatting could be read out loud.
- **Replies arrive in one piece** — Longer answers come through as a single, smooth turn instead of arriving in pieces.
- **Your replies get through** — Fixed cases where a co-host's turn could come out garbled or not at all.
- **Interrupting works better** — Cut in and your co-hosts stop promptly, even if you've switched to another browser tab.
- **Your microphone recovers on its own** — If your mic drops out, it now comes back by itself instead of needing you to restart.
- **You always get a reply** — Fixed a case where answering a co-host could be met with silence.

## Pre-Show Brief
Plan the show before you start it.

_📍 Set it up in the studio's Pre-Show Brief panel_

- **Brief in pages** — Break your brief into pages and step through them during the show, so your co-hosts move with you.
- **Move on when you're ready** — Press Next and the co-hosts make the turn on air, instead of finishing the old topic first.
- **Switch it off without losing it** — Turn the whole brief off for a show and keep it saved for next time.
